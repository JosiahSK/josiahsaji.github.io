// 01 — system overview

System Architecture

The agricultural tilling robot prototype integrates a rear-wheel-drive electric drivetrain with a front-mounted two-stroke rototiller implement. The system combines an embedded microcontroller for low-level motor drive PWM and remote override sequencing, a ceramic patch antenna GPS module for field data acquisition, a dedicated 24V LiFePO4 battery subsystem for powertrain delivery with regulated logic rails, and a 2.4 GHz RF/Bluetooth teleoperation receiver for manual override.

Verification Status: Hardware Prototype & Validation Plan

Custom mild-steel chassis, 24V rear drivetrain, and LiFePO4 battery power system fabricated and bench-tested; submitted to Kerala Startup Mission YIP 7.0 with experimental field trials planned for furrow depth calibration in soft soil.

// 03 — challenges

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge 01
Power Delivery & Inductive Noise Isolation

High in-rush currents and back-EMF from the 24V drive motors coupled with two-stroke engine vibrations caused voltage dips and resets on the 5V logic bus.

Solution →

Migrated the power architecture to a dedicated high-discharge 24V LiFePO4 battery pack, separated the motor power bus from the logic bus using dedicated DC-DC buck regulators with common ground, and added TVS diodes and filter capacitors across motor leads.

Challenge 02
GPS Heading Drift at Low Crawl Speeds

Standard consumer GPS modules produce noisy heading estimates when traversing agricultural plots at slow tilling speeds (0.3–0.6 m/s), leading to erratic steering corrections.

Solution →

GPS coordinates logged with ~3 m observed drift at 0.3 m/s crawl speeds; planned EKF sensor fusion with digital compass and IMU to filter heading.

Challenge 03
Traction Management on Damp Soft Soil

When tilling damp Kerala soil, the active cultivator drum generates severe counter-torque, pushing the robot backward and causing drive wheels to dig in and lose grip.

Solution →

Shifted battery and chassis center of mass forward over the rear drive axle, fitted aggressive agricultural cleated tires, and tuned differential drive torque profiles to counteract tilling drag.
