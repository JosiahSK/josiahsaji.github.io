About Me

🤖 Robotics ⚡ Embedded 🛰 ROS2 🌾 AgriTech

Final-year committee member & Secretary of the Robotics Society at Saintgits College, Kerala.

I specialize in ROS2, embedded systems, and autonomous robotics — turning ideas into machines that operate in real environments. My work spans hardware-software integration, sensor fusion, and power system design.

My current research interests include autonomous navigation, agricultural robotics, and intelligent sensing. I'm passionate about building systems that solve genuine problems — not just proof-of-concept demos.

Beyond engineering, I lead technical workshops, coordinate with industry professionals, and mentor peers in robotics fundamentals and ROS2 development.
