02 🦾

Quadruped Robot

Problem

Wheeled robots cannot navigate uneven, cluttered, or obstacle-dense terrain. A bio-inspired legged platform is needed for scenarios where wheels simply cannot go.

Approach

Designed and built a 12-DOF quadruped with servo-actuated legs. Implemented inverse kinematics for precise leg positioning and multiple gait patterns (trot, walk). Integrated with ROS2 for teleoperation and autonomous navigation planning.
